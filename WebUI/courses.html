Mark order

S = start, F = finish, 1- = marks. S can be used again to cross the start line mid-race (S123S1234F). Use spaces between numbers above 9 (S 1 10 2 F). Leave blank for S, 1, 2, ... F.

New course

A template is placed at the map centre with its first leg into the wind (the Wind menu's direction, else the live wind, else north): 1 km first leg for the triangles and trapezoids, 0.1 km for slalom; start/finish lines 50/25 m (slalom 15/10 m). Move, turn or resize it with Transform.
