Capital Cup 2027

Lake Burley Griffin, Canberra, Australia
Saturday 3 & Sunday 4 April 2027

About

The Capital Cup is a windsurfing regatta featuring marathon-style racing around landmarks on Lake Burley Griffin. Compete over one or two days with 2 races each day (4 races total). Your best 2 results count — drop your 2 worst.

Entry is $20. Prizes will include a (cheap!) trophy
The Capital Cup is back for its second year! Come along and have fun in the spirit of windsurfing, 50% racing, 50% fun!

Eligible Boards

Elegible boards are Windsurfer LT or Windsurfer one-design with 5.7m regatta sail or the new 5m class sail.

Eligible Sailors

You need to get a WCAA member with insurance. See https://www.windsurfer-class-aus.com/ and click the "Join the Windsurfer Class" if you aren't already a member. The ClubSpot registration (once open) should only allow you to register if you are a member.
If you don't have an Australian Sailing (AS) sailing number, the Canberra Yacht Club has kindly offered to provide AS SailPasses to competitors free of charge.

Tracking

We will be supplying GPS trackers for entrants which will make scoring simpler. You can also optionally use your own phone for tracking - see https://wstracker.org for details and how to install the tracker on your own device.
Spectators (both local and remote) will be able to view the races in real-time. See links below for the proposed courses and live tracking web interface.

Water Quality

There can sometimes be issues with algae in the lake. Check water quality at https://lbgweather.au (water quality map at the bottom of the page). See also the NCA water quality page. There are some areas closed in the eastern part of the lake at times; courses will be designed to avoid any closed areas.

WhatsApp Chat

We have a Windsurfer LT Canberra WhatsApp group. Message Andrew Tridgell on 0412 666 929 to ask to be added. Please join that group if you plan on attending as we will be doing updates there.

Venue

This year the event will be hosted at the Canberra Yacht Club (CYC). Briefings will be held at the CYC and we will meet at the CYC for the start of each race. Overnight board storage will be available at the CYC on the Saturday night.
