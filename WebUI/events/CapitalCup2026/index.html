Capital Cup 2026

Lake Burley Griffin, Canberra, Australia
Saturday 11 & Sunday 12 April 2026

About

The Capital Cup is a windsurfing regatta featuring marathon-style racing around landmarks on Lake Burley Griffin. Compete over one or two days with 2 races each day (4 races total). Your best 2 results count — drop your 2 worst.

Entry is free. Prizes will include a (cheap!) trophy
As this is the first year we are running this event and it is being organised quite late you should not expect a perfectly polished event. Come along and have fun in the spirit of windsurfing, 50% racing, 50% fun!

Eligible Boards

Elegible boards are Windsurfer LT or Windsurfer one-design with 5.7m regatta sail or the new 5m class sail.

Eligible Sailors

You need to get a WCAA member with insurance. See https://www.windsurfer-class-aus.com/ and click the "Join the Windsurfer Class" if you aren't already a member. The clubspot registration link below should only allow you to register if you are a member.

Tracking

We will be supplying GPS trackers for entrants which will make scoring simpler. You can also optionally use your own phone for tracking - see https://wstracker.org for details and how to install the tracker on your own device.
Spectators (both local and remote) will be able to view the races in real-time. See links below for the proposed courses and live tracking web interface.

Water Quality

There can sometimes be issues with algae in the lake. See https://www.nca.gov.au/environment/lake-burley-griffin/water-quality for the latest information. There are some areas closed to swimming at the moment but open to sailing.

WhatsApp Chat

We have a Windsurfer LT Canberra WhatsApp group. Message Andrew Tridgell on 0412 666 929 to ask to be added. Please join that group if you plan on attending as we will be doing updates there.

Still baking ...

This event is being organised quite late (only 1 month to go). I haven't locked in a support boat yet, but hope to get that organised. It's a free event, and you should expect to get your moneys worth!

Canberra Marathon Festival

Also note that the Canberra Marathon Festival is the same weekend, there could be crowds and some of them may be cheering the runners instead of us windsurfers. It may be tricky with parking on Sunday morning in particular. More on the WhatsApp chat when we know it.
