NOTICE OF RACE

Capital Cup 2026
Saturday 11 & Sunday 12 April 2026
Lake Burley Griffin, Canberra, Australia

1. Rules

  1. The regatta will be governed by the rules as defined in The Racing Rules of Sailing (RRS) 2025-2028, the rules of the Windsurfer Class Association of Australia (WCAA), and this Notice of Race.

2. Organising Authority

  1. The organising authority is the Windsurfer Class Association of Australia (WCAA), ACT division.

3. Eligibility and Entry

  1. The regatta is open to:
    1. Windsurfer LT boards with 5.7m regatta sail or 5m class sail.
    2. Windsurfer One-Design boards with 5.7m regatta sail or 5m class sail.
  2. All competitors must be current financial members of the WCAA with appropriate insurance. Membership is available at www.windsurfer-class-aus.com.
  3. Entry is free of charge. Registration is via ClubSpot at theclubspot.com/register/regatta/ji2NBpKH5G/class.

4. Schedule

  1. TimeSaturday 11 AprilSunday 12 April
    10:30Competitors briefingCompetitors briefing
    ~11:00Race 1Race 3
    AfternoonRace 2Race 4
  2. Race times may be adjusted depending on wind conditions.
  3. No race will start after 16:00 on either day.

5. Racing Format

  1. The regatta will consist of up to 4 marathon-style races around landmarks on Lake Burley Griffin.
  2. A minimum of 1 race is required to constitute a series.

6. Scoring

  1. The Low Point scoring system of RRS Appendix A will apply.
  2. Each competitor's best 2 results will count; their 2 worst results will be discarded. If fewer than 3 races are completed, no results will be discarded.

7. Venue

  1. Racing will take place on Lake Burley Griffin, Canberra. Briefings will be held on shore near the launch area at Yarralumla Bay.
  2. Competitors should be aware that the Canberra Marathon Festival is on the same weekend; parking near the lake may be limited, particularly on Sunday morning.

8. GPS Tracking

  1. GPS trackers will be supplied to competitors. Competitors may also use their own phone with the Windsurfer Tracker app (see wstracker.org).
  2. Live tracking will be available for spectators at wstracker.org.

9. Safety

  1. All competitors shall wear a personal flotation device (PFD) at all times while on the water.
  2. Competitors are responsible for their own safety and for deciding whether conditions are suitable for them to race.
  3. Competitors should check current water quality advisories at www.nca.gov.au/environment/lake-burley-griffin/water-quality before racing.

10. Prizes

  1. Prizes will be awarded to the top finishers in the series. This is a low-key event; expect a (cheap!) trophy.

11. Further Information

  1. For further information contact Andrew Tridgell on 0412 666 929 or join the Windsurfer LT Canberra WhatsApp group.
