GT06 Battery

Per-unit battery model from gt06_calibration.json: offset = voltage-divider bias (reads high +), R = internal resistance (both solid). Capacity/Wh are nominal-anchored estimates from a shallow discharge (relative ordering meaningful, absolute soft pending a deep discharge). Max time = Wh / per-mode power: track ~0.43 W, idle ~0.28 W (* preliminary — idle keeps the LTE link up so it's most of tracking; GPS-off saves little), sleep pending a clean off-charge MODE5 night. Grey rows = defaults (unseen unit); orange = uncertain fit.
